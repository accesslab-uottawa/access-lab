About the study

Do you live with a disability, chronic illness, a neurodivergent condition, a mental health condition, or another experience that has shaped how you interact with healthcare, biomedical or clinical research, or education? Your story will help train future members of this ecosystem — biomedical researchers, clinical researchers, and healthcare providers — at the University of Ottawa.

Led by the Faculty of Medicine as part of course MED5114 (Diverse Perspectives in Biomedical Research and Healthcare Systems), this project is collecting narratives from individuals with lived experience of disability, chronic illness, neurodivergent conditions, or other health-related realities.

Who can participate?

  • 18 years of age or older
  • Able to communicate in French or English
  • Lived or living experience of a disability, chronic illness, neurodivergent condition, or related health experience

What participation involves

  • A 45–90 minute interview (in person, by phone, or online), or another accessible format of your choice
  • Fully voluntary, confidential, and flexible participation
  • You decide how your story is used and can review it before it's shared
  • Compensation is provided for your time and expertise

Share your experience your way

Accessibility is a core principle of this project. You can share your experience in whatever way works best for you. Accommodations and communication supports will be provided wherever possible.

  • An interview
  • A written narrative
  • An audio or video recording
  • Photographs with a written explanation
  • Assisted communication technology
  • Another accessible format
