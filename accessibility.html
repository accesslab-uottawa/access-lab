Accessibility statement

Accessibility is the centre of our work, and that includes this website. We want everyone to be able to find, read and use the information here.

Our standard

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. This goes beyond the WCAG 2.0 Level AA requirement under Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

What we built in

  • Works with screen readers, with clear headings, page regions and descriptive links
  • Every feature works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline
  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page
  • Text and background colours meet WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode
  • Follows your device's light or dark setting and high contrast mode
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content
  • Pages reflow to a single column on small screens
  • No moving content, time limits or autoplaying media
  • Colour is never the only way information is shown
  • Plain language and short sections
  • Typefaces designed for readability: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Lexend
  • The same navigation in the same place on every page

Ask for another format

We can provide the information on this site in another format, such as large print, plain text, an audio recording, or in French. Email us and tell us the page and the format that works for you.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site does not work for you, we want to know. It helps to include:

  • The page and what you were trying to do
  • What happened
  • The device, browser and any assistive technology you use

Email Nadine.WiperBergeron@uottawa.ca with the subject line "Website accessibility".

Known limitations

  • This site is currently in English. To receive any information in French, email us.
  • Some pages we link to, such as University of Ottawa pages and LinkedIn profiles, are managed by others and may not meet the same standard.
